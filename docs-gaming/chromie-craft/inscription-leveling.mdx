# Inscription Leveling 1-450 (WotLK)

Mill in stacks of 5, craft the listed recipe until the next tier unlocks, and use **Minor Inscription Research** (75+) and **Northrend Inscription Research** (385+) on cooldown for free skill points. Every scroll/glyph also needs **Light Parchment** from the trainer vendor.

Click the **Herb sources** row under each tier for plant + zone info.

| Level | Craft | Recipe | Materials per craft |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1 → 75** | ~30× | <img src="https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/small/inv_scroll_01.jpg" width="20" /> Scroll of Stamina | 1 Moonglow Ink + 1 Light Parchment |
| **75 → 125** | ~25× | <img src="https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/small/inv_scroll_01.jpg" width="20" /> Scroll of Stamina II | 1 Midnight Ink + 1 Light Parchment |
| **125 → 205** | ~40× | <img src="https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/small/inv_scroll_01.jpg" width="20" /> Scroll of Strength III/IV | 1 Lion's Ink + 1 Light Parchment |
| **205 → 260** | ~30× | <img src="https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/small/inv_inscription_armorscroll01.jpg" width="20" /> Armor Vellum, then Scroll V | 1 Jadefire Ink + 1 Light Parchment |
| **260 → 290** | ~20× | <img src="https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/small/inv_scroll_02.jpg" width="20" /> Scroll of Agility VI | 1 Celestial Ink + 1 Light Parchment |
| **290 → 330** | ~25× | <img src="https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/small/inv_inscription_armorscroll02.jpg" width="20" /> Armor Vellum II, then Scroll VI | 1 Shimmering Ink + 1 Light Parchment |
| **330 → 385** | ~45× | <img src="https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/small/inv_inscription_inkblack02.jpg" width="20" /> Darkflame Ink / Scroll VII | 2 Ethereal Ink + 1 Light Parchment |
| **385 → 450** | ~70× | <img src="https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/small/inv_inscription_inkpurple01.jpg" width="20" /> Mysterious Tarot → trainer glyphs | 1–3 Ink of the Sea + 1 Light Parchment |
