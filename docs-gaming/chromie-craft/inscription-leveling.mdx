# Inscription Leveling 1-450 (WotLK)

A condensed leveling path for **Scribe** on a 3.3.5a server. Each tier lists the **skill range**, the **recipe to spam**, the **ink** it consumes, and the **herbs** that mill into the required pigment (1 pigment per 5 herbs on average; ~2.5 pigments per 5 herbs of the higher type).

> **Always mill, never buy inks at vendor cost.** Mill in stacks of 5. Save extra pigments — higher-tier herbs also produce small amounts of the next-tier pigment which you'll use for off-hands and shoulder enchants later.

## Prep before you start

- Visit a **Scribe trainer** in any capital city (or Dalaran later).
- Train **Apprentice → Master** as you hit each cap (50 / 125 / 200 / 275 / 350 / 425).
- Buy a stack of **Light Parchment** from the trainer's vendor — every Scroll/Glyph needs one.
- At skill 75, train and use **Minor Inscription Research** on cooldown (free skill point + recipe learn).
- At skill 385, train and use **Northrend Inscription Research** on cooldown.

## Tier-by-tier path

### 1 → 75 — Moonglow Ink

- **Mill:** Peacebloom, Silverleaf, Earthroot → **Alabaster Pigment** → **Moonglow Ink**
- **Craft:** **Scroll of Stamina** (or Scroll of Agility / Intellect / Spirit / Strength — all yellow→green across this range)
- ~40–60 herbs total.

### 75 → 125 — Midnight Ink

- **Mill:** Mageroyal, Briarthorn, Swiftthistle, Bruiseweed, Stranglekelp → **Dusky Pigment** → **Midnight Ink**
- **Craft:** **Scroll of Stamina II** / **Scroll of Strength II**
- Learn **Minor Inscription Research** at 75 and run it whenever it's off cooldown until ~125.

### 125 → 175 — Lion's Ink (round 1)

- **Mill:** Wild Steelbloom, Grave Moss, Kingsblood, Liferoot → **Golden Pigment** → **Lion's Ink**
- **Craft:** **Scroll of Protection III** or **Scroll of Stamina III**

### 175 → 205 — Lion's Ink (round 2)

- Same herbs / ink as above.
- **Craft:** **Scroll of Strength IV** / **Scroll of Stamina IV**

### 205 → 260 — Jadefire Ink

- **Mill:** Wintersbite, Fadeleaf, Goldthorn, Khadgar's Whisker → **Emerald Pigment** → **Jadefire Ink**
- **Craft:** **Armor Vellum** (180–210) then **Scroll of Strength V** / **Scroll of Stamina V**
- Khadgar's Whisker has the best pigment yield in this band.

### 260 → 290 — Celestial Ink

- **Mill:** Arthas' Tears, Sungrass, Blindweed, Firebloom, Purple Lotus, Ghost Mushroom, Gromsblood → **Violet Pigment** → **Celestial Ink**
- **Craft:** **Scroll of Agility VI** / **Scroll of Intellect VI**

### 290 → 330 — Shimmering Ink

- **Mill:** Dreamfoil, Mountain Silversage, Plaguebloom, Golden Sansam, Icecap → **Silvery Pigment** → **Shimmering Ink**
- **Craft:** **Scroll of Strength VI** then **Armor Vellum II** at 325
- Dreamfoil mills the best of this set.

### 330 → 385 — Ethereal Ink

- **Mill:** Felweed, Dreaming Glory, Ragveil, Terocone, Ancient Lichen, Netherbloom, Nightmare Vine, Mana Thistle → **Nether Pigment** → **Ethereal Ink**
- **Craft:** **Darkflame Ink** off-hand recipe, or **Scroll of Stamina VII** / **Scroll of Intellect VII**
- Felweed and Dreaming Glory are the cheapest sources.

### 385 → 450 — Ink of the Sea

- **Mill:** Goldclover, Tiger Lily, Talandra's Rose, Adder's Tongue, Lichbloom, Icethorn, Deadnettle, Fire Leaf → **Azure Pigment** → **Ink of the Sea**
- **Craft path:**
  - 385–405: **Scroll of Stamina VIII** / **Scroll of Strength VIII**
  - 405–425: **Mysterious Tarot** or **Armor Vellum III**
  - 425–450: any **trainer-yellow glyph** — pick the cheapest ink-only glyphs for your faction's AH.
- Run **Northrend Inscription Research** every 20h from 385 onward — it's a guaranteed skill point and learns a random glyph you can sell.

## Approximate total materials

| Tier | Herbs needed (avg) |
| --- | --- |
| 1 → 75 | ~50 Peacebloom/Silverleaf/Earthroot |
| 75 → 125 | ~60 Mageroyal/Briarthorn |
| 125 → 205 | ~140 Kingsblood/Liferoot/Wild Steelbloom |
| 205 → 260 | ~110 Khadgar's Whisker/Goldthorn/Fadeleaf |

| 330 → 385 | ~160 Felweed/Dreaming Glory |

Plan for **~900 herbs total** if you're milling efficiently and supplementing with Research cooldowns.

## Gold-making tips while you level

- **Save every Northrend pigment** — green/violet/burnt/silvery/sapphire/ruby/indigo by-products go into **off-hand books** and **shoulder enchants (Inscriptions of the …)** that sell well.
- **Don't vendor low-level glyphs** — post the popular ones (Glyph of Blocking, Glyph of Rapid Charge, etc.) on the AH.
- **Buy herbs in bulk on Tuesdays** after raid reset when farmers dump stacks cheap.
- **Make Darkmoon cards** once you have steady Ink of the Sea — Greatness cards are still BiS trinkets for most melee/casters in early WotLK content.
